About T-Creates

T-Creates is my personal crochet business that I've built at the starting of my first year in T.A. Marryshow Community College (TAMCC)

I enjoy crocheting because it makes me feel peaceful and comfortable. Knowing that my creations can make other people happy is the best reward, and it makes me enjoy crocheting even more.

My Interests

  • Creating handmade items
  • Making cute designs
  • Learning new crochet techniques

My Goal

My goal is to continue improving my crochet skills, create more unique pieces and eventually grow T-Creates into a successful handmade business.
