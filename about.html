About T-Creates

My Story

T-Creates is my personal crochet business, which I started during my first year at TAMCC.

I enjoy crocheting because it makes me feel peaceful and comfortable. Knowing that my creations make people happy is one of the best rewards.

My Interests

  • Crochet
  • Creating handmade items
  • Making cute designs
  • Learning new crochet techniques

My Goal

My goal is to continue improving my crochet skills, create more unique pieces, and eventually grow T-Creates into a successful handmade business.
